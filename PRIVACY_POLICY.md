# Privacy Policy — Tennis Serve Analyzer

_Last updated: 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Tennis Serve Analyzer** app ("the app",
"we") handles your data. Please replace the contact details and hosting URL
before publishing, and review with a lawyer if in doubt.

## Summary

- The app analyzes your tennis serve **entirely on your device**.
- Your **videos and analysis results are stored only on your device** and are
  **never uploaded** to us or any server.
- We do **not** collect accounts, names, emails, or location.
- The app is free and shows **ads** (Google AdMob), which may process device
  identifiers as described below.

## Camera and microphone

The app uses the **camera** to record and analyze your serve, and the
**microphone** to capture audio in recorded videos. All processing (pose
estimation, ball/racket detection, scoring) happens **locally on your device**
using on-device models. Recorded videos and the serve history are saved in the
app's private storage and can be deleted at any time in **Settings → Clear
history**.

## Data we collect

We (the developer) do **not** collect or receive any of your personal data,
videos, or analysis results. There is no user account and no server backend.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads via **Google AdMob**. To serve ads, Google may collect and
process certain data, including a device advertising identifier, IP address, and
app interactions, in accordance with Google's policies. In regions where it is
required (e.g., the EEA/UK), the app shows a **consent dialog** and only serves
personalized ads if you consent.

- Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Crash reporting (if enabled)

If crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics) is enabled, anonymized diagnostic data
(device model, OS version, stack traces) may be collected to fix crashes. No
video or personal content is included.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data
from children.

## Your choices

- Delete your serve videos/history at any time (Settings → Clear history).
- Manage ad personalization via your device's ad settings and the in-app consent
  dialog.
- Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
